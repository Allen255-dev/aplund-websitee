Aplund website

How to open: double-click index.html (works in any browser, no setup needed).

Folder layout:
  index.html     page structure and text
  css/style.css  all styling and colours (edit the variables at the top)
  js/main.js     product list, filters, WhatsApp order and quote form
  images/        put product photos and logo here

To edit products or prices: open js/main.js and change the P list near the top.
To change the WhatsApp number: edit NUM at the top of js/main.js (format 234XXXXXXXXXX).

To put it online: upload the whole folder to a host such as Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages or any cPanel hosting, and connect a domain.
