COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, customization, and nationwide delivery.

How much do custom mirrors cost?

Our custom LED mirrors start from ₦25,000 to ₦30,000 for compact and standard formats (such as the viral Insta Mirror or Krusty Pop Art). Larger full-length mirrors and custom drip furniture (like sneaker cabinets) are quoted based on exact dimensions and hardware. We confirm a fixed written quote before payment.

How safe is delivery outside Lagos & Abuja?

We deliver nationwide to all 36 states! For inter-state transit, all pieces are packed with multi-layer high-density bubble foam, reinforced corner guards, and encased in heavy-duty custom wooden shipping crates to guarantee zero transit breakage.

Can I personalize it with my own Instagram handle or business logo?

Yes, 100%! You can specify any Instagram handle, business name, custom phrase, or send us your vector/PNG logo file on WhatsApp. We will integrate it into the 3D design proof for your confirmation.

How does the mirror plug in and turn on?

Every mirror includes a high-efficiency power adapter that plugs into any standard 220V wall socket. We also offer options for integrated smart touch sensors on the glass, dimmer controllers, or direct wall-switch hardwiring.

Is Aplund an officially registered business?

Yes! Aplund is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of Nigeria. You can view our certificate, real customer reviews, and finished video builds on our verified TikTok page (@aplundcompany).
